إزاي تلعب

&quot;Save the Animal Kingdom&quot; is a tapping game where players control an animal character trying to escape a lava-filled world. By tapping on either the left or right side of the screen, the character moves and jumps from leaf to leaf, using plants as a way to avoid the dangerous lava below. The goal is to guide the character to safety and save the animal kingdom.
