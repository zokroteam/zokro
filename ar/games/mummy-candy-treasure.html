إزاي تلعب

Imagine that such a mummy is alive and that she would again be a man she needs to eat a lot of magic sweets. Today in the game Mummy Candy Treasure you will help her in this. Our character will stand on the sand, and under it will be located candy and other items. You need to pull out sweets with the help of a hook and in no case can you touch other objects.
