إزاي تلعب

Are you ready to run your own Halloween park? My Halloween Park is a 3D simulation game that lets you create a spooky and fun amusement park for visitors. You can collect ghosts and load them into the haunted house, where visitors will pay you to enter and experience the horror. You can use the money to unlock and upgrade more entertainment facilities and play areas, such as pumpkin patches, candy shops, and ghost trains. You can also increase your ghost-carrying capacity and gardening seed capacity to make your park more attractive. My Halloween Park is a game that will immerse you in the spirit of Halloween.
