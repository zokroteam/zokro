Stack City Online is a merge game where you can build your own city using stacked scaffolding, shops, and buildings! Enjoy the freedom to design your ideal city and watch every building generate money, allowing you to explore new land and purchase more scaffolding! Simply drag the scaffolding onto the grid and stack them to create a higher building. Please note that some blocks are color coded, so you can only build things of the same color! Can you build your own stack city?
