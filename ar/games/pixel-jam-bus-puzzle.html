إزاي تلعب

Pixel Jam Bus Puzzle is a colorful sorting puzzle where tiny passengers form playful pixel pictures. Send vehicles into limited parking spaces, match their colors to waiting passengers, and clear each picture. Plan your moves around vehicle capacity and the colors needed next. Explore 1,000 puzzle levels with progressive difficulty and occasional easier stages. Use Clear and Shuffle helpers, pickup speed upgrades, and optional ad rewards. Play with a mouse or touch controls in portrait or landscape. Progress is saved in the current browser.
