Jump your ball upward and survive as long as you can in this fast-paced arcade game! Control a bouncy ball and help it jump higher while avoiding sharp spikes and dangerous obstacles. Perfect timing and quick reflexes are the key to success. Each jump becomes more challenging as the speed increases, testing your skills and focus. With simple one-tap controls and endless vertical gameplay, this casual hyper-arcade game is easy to play but hard to master. How high can you jump without hitting the spikes?
