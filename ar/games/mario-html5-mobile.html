إزاي تلعب

Meet the updated version of Mario's adventures in the Super Mario Html5 game. The plumber will again overcome various obstacles, break gold blocks, jump on turtles and evil stray mushrooms. Collect coins, for them you can buy an additional life to the three that are already available. Magic mushrooms can be hidden in golden cups. If you eat them, the hero will turn into a super Mario and become much larger. But the first collision with the enemy will return it to its original form again. Beware of carnivorous flowers and jump over water and fire obstacles in Super Mario Html5.
