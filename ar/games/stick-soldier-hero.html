إزاي تلعب

tick Soldier is a free puzzle game. A soldier wants to use his only tool - stick to pass all the ditches, Try your best to build a path by estimating the width of ditches. You can click and hold the screen then adjust the stick to a suitable length. Strech out the soldier's stick and make sure the end of the stick sits right on the second pillar. The soldier will faill if the stick is either too short or too long. So make sure you pull out the right length and keep the soldier safe!
