The dead own the night. Take back the streets. DEADSHIFT: Halloween is a 3D zombie-driving game with 20 missions in a quarantined arena. Smash through the horde, collect supplies, rescue survivors, and race to extraction. Powerful collisions send zombies tumbling into ragdolls, with green splatter and impact-driven limb separation. Choose your vehicle, customize its paint, and upgrade its engine, armor, and nitro. Claim daily rewards and fight to survive all 20 nights.
