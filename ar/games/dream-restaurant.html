How about building the restaurant of your dreams? The cook is ready for duty at the beginning of January. As a waiter, invite customers in. Fulfill your wishes immediately. Grow step by step, starting with such tasks as adding tables, hiring waiters. In the following times, sushi, donat, etc. you can also open restaurants. Start taking the first steps towards building the restaurant chains that adorn your dreams. Have fun with the fun 3D clicker game Dream Restaurant with you!
