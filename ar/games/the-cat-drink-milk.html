إزاي تلعب

Pets such as cats and cats love to drink milk. In game The Cat Drink Milk you will feed various cats with milk. You will do this in a rather original way. A playing field will appear on the screen in front of you on which at the bottom you will see a sitting cat. A bag of milk will hang on a rope above it at a certain height. It can swing from side to side like a pendulum. You will have scissors at your disposal. You will have to guess the moment and cut the rope while the bag of milk is over the cat. Then he will fall into his hands, and you will receive points for it.
