Zombie Mission starts with episode 9 in the underground city. After the human cities in the world were damaged, life began in the underground cities. But the zombies discovered the underground cities and took some hostages. You must use the necessary weapons and vehicles to save them. Also, do not forget to collect the objects in the levels. At the end of the game, 3 different BOSS! will be waiting for you. So don't forget to improve your weapons, life and robot from the MARKET section! (You can do it with the coins you earn between levels.) Characters can jump on walls.
