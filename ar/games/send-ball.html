إزاي تلعب

Even on an even path it is difficult to drive and in the game Send Ball you will be convinced of this. The green ball wants to hit the portal of the same color, it will lead the hero home. But first you have to go through the white labyrinth. Help the character get to the goal, you'll have to try and show skill. The number of collisions is strictly limited, if you exceed the limit, the journey will end.
