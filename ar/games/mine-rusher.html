إزاي تلعب

Precious crystals are scattered on the paths of the maze in the game Mine Rusher, the character must collect them, and he always fails, he does not have time to react to the turns of the path, he falls into the void and wakes up empty - handed over without extraction. You can help an amusing lunatic if you take control in your own hands and guide the hero along the path, collecting pebbles. Get ready for a tight arcade, react to calls instantly, otherwise the game will throw you away. Let the hero with your help pick up the full pockets of gems.
