MOMENTUM is a high-speed endless runner that challenges your reflexes! Control a unique doodle character dashing through evolving worlds&amp;mdash;from the Animal Kingdom to futuristic Alien Realms. Dodge obstacles, collect crowns, and survive the increasing speed. How far can you run before the void consumes you?
