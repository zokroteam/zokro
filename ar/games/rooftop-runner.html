Game Objective The goal in Rooftop Runner is simple: run as far as you can without falling. As you move across rooftops, you must: Jump over gaps between buildings Avoid obstacles and hazards Collect coins scattered across rooftops Stay ahead of danger and survive as long as possible The longer you run, the higher your score becomes. How to Play Playing Rooftop Runner is easy to learn but challenging to master. Basic Controls Move Forward Automatically &amp;ndash; Your character runs automatically. Jump &amp;ndash; Tap or press the jump key to leap across rooftop gaps. Change Direction / Dodge &amp;ndash; Move left or right to avoid obstacles. Collect Coins &amp;ndash; Run through coins to increase your score and rewards. Timing your jumps correctly is important. If you jump too early or too late, you may fall between buildings.
