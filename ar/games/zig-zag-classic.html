Zig Zag classic is an online Kids game, it's playable on all smartphones or tablets, such as iPhone, iPad, Samsung and other Apple and android system. Zig Zag classic is a cool and fast-paced 3D arcade game. The game has only one rolling ball and a variety of curves generated at random. The player has only one operation, change the movement direction of the ball by clicking the screen, and try to get high scores.
