إزاي تلعب

Do you like games easy to understand but difficult to master? This game is for you! You are a small Samurai and you have to survive as long as possible! Jump from floor to floor and avoid the enemies! You can jump as many times as you like, but beware of the enemies, if you touch them, it's over! Ikki Samurai Jump is an addictive game that will test your reflexes, attention and speed. You have to make good jumps and you must stay away from the enemies. How long will you survive?
