إزاي تلعب

Finding Animal is a fun and entertaining observation game where you must quickly find the different animal among a series of similar characters. Each level is a visual challenge that requires focus and keen eyesight. As you progress to higher levels, the number and similarity of characters increase, making it more interesting to find the differences. The game is simple and easy to play but full of challenges, suitable for entertainment and improving observation skills.
