Stickman Archery Master - Archer Puzzle Warrior Be an Archer Hero (Arc-hero) and join the battle. Fight enemies, shoot em up and become an archer legend. But more levels you passed, more legend weapons you could find, and more mystical arrows you could discover on your way.
