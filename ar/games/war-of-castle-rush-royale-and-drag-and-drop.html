War of Castle: Rush Royale and Drag and drop is an addictive drag and drop game. Drag and drop the brave little man to fight the onslaught of bats! Your goal is to destroy all enemies by gently moving your character across the screen. Collect bonuses, upgrade weapons, and evolve to tackle increasingly difficult levels. Demonstrate dexterity and strategic thinking to become a true master of the bat hunt!
