إزاي تلعب

Run together and build a brighter future in Family Dash! Guide two parents sprinting side by side as they collect money along their paths. The parent holding the baby gathers the cash, but obstacles ahead mean quick teamwork is essential. Toss the baby between parents at the right moment to dodge barriers and keep the money flowing. Every decision counts as you balance timing, speed, and strategy. The more money you collect by the finish line, the bigger the future for the baby - unlocking different professions based on your performance. Can your family dash their way to success?
