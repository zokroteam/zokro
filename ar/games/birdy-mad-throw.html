Birdy Mad Throw is a chaotic arcade game where an angry little bird is launched across crazy levels using pure throwing power. Aim, charge, and release to smash obstacles, hit targets, and cause maximum mayhem. Each throw demands timing and precision as physics send Birdy bouncing, spinning, and crashing through the environment. Unlock new stages, face tricky layouts, and chase high scores while mastering the art of the perfect mad throw. Fast, silly, and endlessly replayable fun.
