إزاي تلعب

Merge Food Puzzle is a synthetic puzzle game. What is different from ordinary match-3 games is that successful matches will not disappear and will generate higher-level food. The game has two modes: Merge BBQ and Merge Fruit. Merge BBQ has a variety of barbecues waiting for you to unlock, including corn, cauliflower, fish, squid, and more. Have fun!
