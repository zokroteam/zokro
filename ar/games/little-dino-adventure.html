إزاي تلعب

Platform adventure game, where the little Dinosaur walk through the stages to collect as many small eggs as you can to score many points, tap the little dinosaur's head on the yellow blocks to collect even more eggs to score. Each of the three stages has three large golden eggs, try to pass the stage collecting the three to complete 100%. Jump on the enemies to gain points and destroy them. Play through the arrow keys or touch buttons on your mobile device.
