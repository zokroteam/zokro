In this game, you can create and dress up your own cute anime version known as Chibi! You have tons of options to create your Chibi character however you like. Make a super cute chibi girl or boy. You have clothing options like blouses, pants, skirts, shoes, socks, and ties. It also has earrings, necklaces, hats, hair clips, and even pets. You have 80 colors available to color your Chibi in different ways. Click on the brush to view all options. Choose Color 1 or Color 2 to paint different areas of the chosen part. You can save your creation with full body or avatar mode. It's perfect for creating your original characters or trying to create yourself as a Chibi persona!
