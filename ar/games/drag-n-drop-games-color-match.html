Welcome to Color Match!, the ultimate interactive learning experience for children! This game is designed to help young learners identify and distinguish colors in a fun, engaging way. The goal is simple: Look at the black-and-white objects in the targets and find the matching colored card from the source box. Drag the red card to the apple, the blue card to the whale, and watch the objects come to life with color!
