Baby Taylor Life Diary is a cleaning and pet care game for girls. Taylors mum is going out and she has given Taylor the task of cleaning her room. You need to help Taylor along with it. There are 4 tasks, which are bathing your pet, sorting out the scattered toys, cleaning the dusty bookshelf, and making a nice-looking harpsichord. During the game, you can not only enjoy the game but also learn how to do housework and take care of your pet. Can you make sure that you do all the tasks before your mum comes home?
