إزاي تلعب

he ice cream seller has come to the neighborhood! He has kidnapped your friend and neighbor &quot;Bob&quot; and you've seen it all. It has frozen your best friend with some kind of power, and you have to discover where it takes. What if there are more children like him behind this story? This terrifying ice cream seller is called Rod, and he seems very friendly to young children, but he has an evil plan for them that you will have to discover. All you know is that he will take it inside his ice cream van, but you don't know where. Your mission will be to hide inside his van and solve the mystery of this evil villain. To do this, you will travel through different scenarios and solve the necessary puzzles to save the frozen child.
