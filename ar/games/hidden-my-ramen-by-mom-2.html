Where is my ramen? My mom had to hide my ramen again.I have to find it. Mom will not let me eat ramen, hide my ramen. I want to eat ramen, I want to eat ramen every day. In the game you have to do everything possible to find ramen, and can not let mom find you. This is a very interesting puzzle game, you need to use a variety of props to find ramen, and cook ramen, not only to avoid all kinds of traps, but also to avoid being found by my mother!
