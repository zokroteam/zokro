إزاي تلعب

The Fishercat is an online game where you’ll be helping a cat catch as many fish as possible. Just pick up your diving suit and harpoon and you can start fishing. There will be many types of fish swimming around you, so try to shoot your harpoon where there’ll be as many fish as possible at one place. You’ll have to take care not to touch any medusa, since they can shock you and thus lower your oxygen level. That’s important, since when your oxygen reaches 0, you die. Aside from that you can also pick up many different bonuses, like for instance a fishing net that will help you catch more fish. Try to get the highest score you can.
