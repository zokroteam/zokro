إزاي تلعب

Draw a road is an addictive physics puzzle where you draw the road for a car to reach the finish line. The car cannot pass on its own &amp;mdash; you have to draw bridges, ramps and paths to get it across pits, gaps and obstacles. The car drives exactly where you draw! Each level brings a new challenge: holes, cliffs, boxes and hills. Collect nitro along the way for a speed boost, upgrade your wheels and paint your car any color you like. Dozens of levels with rising difficulty keep things fun and tricky. Simple to start, hard to put down. Perfect for short breaks and great for training spatial thinking.
