إزاي تلعب

Few of us are delighted with our bosses, as a rule, they are not very fond of them, even if they are quite loyal to their subordinates. This is ungrateful human nature. But the boss in our game Beat the Boss is really insufferable. He humiliates his subordinates, burdens them with backbreaking work, and then punishes for failure to comply. Everyone hates him and wants nothing more, nothing less - a cruel death. However, no one wants to go to jail, so they can only kill the hated boss in their own image. Especially for harsh situations, our game was invented, where you can pour out your anger on the virtual boss.
