إزاي تلعب

The objective of this game is simple yet challenging: destroy apart the descending crystal to earn points while ensuring that the canon remains untouched until the game concludes. Your primary tool is your quick-witted Canon, armed and ready to launch projectiles to shatter the incoming crystals into oblivion. But beware, a single collision between the crystal and your Canon will spell the end of the game, so exercise caution and timing to perfect. Features of this game: -High Quality graphics -Game size is minimal (but not compromised the graphics) -Super simple controls.
