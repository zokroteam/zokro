Troll Thief - Stickman Puzzle is a free online puzzle game. In this game, one of the stickman's arms is stretchy. And you can help him drag his hand to reach the things he wants. Note that there are many barriers between you and the target. If you reach the wrong thing, you mignt fail at the level. Which level will you fail at? Hopefully not the first. Good luck!
