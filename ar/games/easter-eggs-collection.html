إزاي تلعب

Collect the easter eggs in this html5 game. Press any eggs to start. Now move the 'mouse or finger tip' on identical adjacent eggs (horizontally, vertically or diagonally). Select at least 3 eggs. Release mouse button to make them match. Each 6th eggs will give a bonus. More than 7 eggs will give you a time bonus. Collect the request eggs within the giving time, or you will lose the game.
