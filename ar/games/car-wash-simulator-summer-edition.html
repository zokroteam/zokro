إزاي تلعب

Car Wash Simulator is a relaxing simulator where you scrub, hose, dry and polish cars to perfection. Work through a daily lineup of customers, cleaning each car through four stages: hose, sponge, polish, towel. Complete all four to finish a car and move to the next, including rarer, higher-value vehicles. Spend your earnings on four upgrades: Cash Multiplier, Cars Per Day, Cleaning Speed, and Rare Car Chance. Simple, tap-friendly gameplay for quick sessions or longer relaxing runs.
