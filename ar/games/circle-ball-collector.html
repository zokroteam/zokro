إزاي تلعب

In the game Circle Ball Collector, you will also become a collector and the object of your collection will be colorful balls that frolic on the playing field. At the bottom, you will see three colored circles. By clicking on one of them, you will make the balls of the same color attract to you. But watch out for a gray nondescript object that will flicker in front of the trap circles. If even one of the balls collides with him, you will lose.
