إزاي تلعب

Paper Stick Figures is a fast-paced tower defense battle drawn on a notebook page. Build your hand-sketched army, deploy stick soldiers, tanks, aircraft, and heavy weapons, then hold the line against waves of enemy stick figures. Every level turns simple paper doodles into a chaotic battlefield filled with missiles, explosions, and tactical upgrades. Strengthen your base, improve your troops, collect rewards, and survive each wave with clever timing and firepower.
