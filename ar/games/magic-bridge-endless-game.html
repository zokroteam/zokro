إزاي تلعب

Magic Bridge is a skill game. Take control of one of the many cat characters and try to stay alive on a bridge that keeps moving upward. The bridge will point downward depending on where you stand, so you have to be quick on your paws to keep a steady balance. Avoid all enemies, pick up all the coins and unlock more characters who have different strengths. Jump on the bridge and experience this endless fun! How to play: Move on the platform and avoid touching the enemies.
