إزاي تلعب

INKAGEDDON: the doodles have had enough. Fight through 5 notebook worlds -- Fresh Page, Graph Paper, Sticky Note, Blueprint, Midnight Chalk -- each with its own boss. Pick from 3 heroes and build a run from 6 weapons that merge to tier 3 and unlock secret evolutions. Survive ink blots, mites, and highlighter hornets, bank Ink for permanent shop upgrades, chase 12 badges, and fill the Codex. Three difficulty modes (Chill, Normal, Nightmare) plus a seeded Daily Doodle run. Zero-click start: WASD/arrows/drag to move, space or tap to dash, and go.
