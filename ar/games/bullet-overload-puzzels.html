إزاي تلعب

Bullet Overload is the newest puzzle from makers of Laser Overload and Laser Overload 2. The goal is to set all the elements of the puzzle to point the guns at the bad guys and eliminate all of them! How to play&gt; The gameplay is very simple yet entertaining. Tap on elements to rotate them. When the puzzle is completed the &quot;Shoot&quot; button will blink. Tap on it to fire the bullet! If you're stuck you can always get a hint. Gather cash to unlock different guns. Change your appearance the way you like best!
