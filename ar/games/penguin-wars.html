إزاي تلعب

Penguin Wars is a game in which you play as a penguin trying to conquer Antarctica. You are going to do this by pushing all of the other penguins off of their icebergs. You have to go through all of the areas armed with nothing but your bazooka filled with snowballs, as well as your knowledge in spacing your shots.
