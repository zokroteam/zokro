إزاي تلعب

Ultimate Disc, also and originally known as ultimate Frisbee, is a low-contact team sport played with a flying disc (Frisbee). The object of the game is to score points by passing the disc to a team member in the opposing team's end zone. Players may not move about the field while holding the disc. Frisbee sport is a game based on the rules of golf (referred to by disc golfers as &quot;ball and stick golf&quot;). It uses discs smaller and denser than an ultimate disc. The discs are thrown towards a target, which serves as the &quot;hole&quot;. The official targets are metal baskets with hanging chains to catch the discs.
