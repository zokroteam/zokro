Spectators of races on pristine asphalt courses always secretly wish they could trade places with a rally driver for a day to feel the exhilaration of top speed and the rush of adrenaline enjoyed only by f1 drivers. This Grand Prix race simulator is for those who believe they have what it to succeed in GT Motorsport. Find the answer to a burning query: &quot;Do I have what it necessary to win a world title in asphalt rallying?&quot; Put your plan into action immediately!
