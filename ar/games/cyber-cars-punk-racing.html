إزاي تلعب

Cyber Cars Punk Racing is a 3D car driving game with futuristic cars and theme. There are super vehicles, neon city, and many game modes. 16 supercars, 60 racetracks, 2 players, 1 free ride area, Hot Pursuit area, and a battlefield are waiting for you. This game has a vehicle purchase and development system, and you can race against artificial intelligence or drive freely. Game Modes Career There are many areas in which you can test yourself in career mode. These areas are &quot;Race&quot;, &quot;Against Time&quot;, &quot;Stunt&quot;, &quot;CheckPoint&quot;, and &quot;Fly Parkour&quot; respectively. When the race is completed, you receive stars based on your completion degree. You can get a maximum of 160 stars in career mode. There are 12 racetracks in &quot;Race&quot; mode. You race with 7 Artificial Intelligence and if you can enter the top 3 when the race is over, you have the right to move on to the next race track. When the race is over, you earn money according to the time you complete the race. In addition, the drift points and flight points you earn in the race also give you money. In the &quot;Time Against&quot; mode, you race against time. There are 12 racetracks in total. When you reach the finish line, you rate it based on when you reached it. Get star ratings here. With these star ratings, you will be able to buy new and supercars. In &quot;Stunt&quot; mode, you need to reach the finish line by performing stunts on 12 specially designed racetracks. Reaching the finish line in time by staying on the track on challenging race tracks takes talent. In &quot;Check Point&quot; mode, you need to try to reach the finish line by overcoming obstacles on 12 specially designed racetracks. In &quot;Fly Parkour&quot; mode, you need to try to stay off the road by reaching incredible speeds. You can get new vehicles with the stars you get in &quot;Career&quot;. Also, with the money you earn, you can buy new vehicles and increase engine power. Free Ride Get ready for free rides in a big city. The free mode area contains large buildings, wide streets, stunt areas, and many mysterious areas. There are 60 gems hidden in the city. By collecting these gems, you can have new vehicles. The &quot;Reward&quot; table opens every 180 seconds. In this &quot;Reward&quot; table, you earn money based on your drift and flight points. If you wait 180 seconds, you will get good rewards. When the number of keys reaches a certain level, the vehicle is opened for automatic use. Hot Chase Police are chasing vehicles and want to catch you? Ready for this action-packed chase? In this mode, we have the &quot;HP&quot; value. Damages we take while fleeing from the cops decrease our &quot;HP&quot; value. So try to stay away from the cops. Be careful, the cops will always be after you. When your &quot;HP&quot; value is 0, you are caught. Cops arrest you. To top up your &quot;HP&quot;, simply press the &quot;Extra HP&quot; button. This button will appear briefly when hp is 0. Battle Arena Get ready for an action-packed battlefield. A total of 8 racers will show their skills on the specially designed battlefield. There are 3 special abilities: &quot;Damage X5&quot;, &quot;HP +25&quot;, &quot;SHIELD&quot;. You can crush your opponents by collecting these skills. The last man standing wins. This mode is a mode in which vehicles try to crash and explode. Each vehicle has 100 HP and when the HP value drops to 0, the vehicle explodes. The competitor with 0 HP is withdrawn from the battle. Your HP + 20 increases when you detonate another. There are ranking rewards at the end of the battle. You must be in the top 3 to receive these ranking rewards. Lets! Get on the battlefield and roar like a lion. Two Players (2P Versus) You can compete with your friends on the same computer with a half screen. Prove who's the better driver.
