إزاي تلعب

Turn Over Master is a thrilling new arcade game with a 3D art style and driving elements. Click the mouse to accelerate the truck. When the car is driving to a high place, you need to control the car to accurately fall on the platform track to avoid the car Crash, the track in the game is very complicated, similar to the roller coaster track in an amusement park, the car will fly down from high in the sky many times, and you also need to collect the gold coins on the track. These gold coins can be used to buy more trucks, are you ready?
