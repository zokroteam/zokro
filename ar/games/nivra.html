إزاي تلعب

In a world shattered by endless wars, the land has turned into a barren wasteland ruled by powerful tank factions. Cities have fallen, resources are scarce, and survival depends on the strength of your armor and the precision of your aim. The few who remain fight not for glory but for control of the last fuel and metal that keep their machines alive. You are a lone survivor who stumbles upon an abandoned war factory buried under the sands. Inside, you find an old but powerful tank, forgotten by time. With nothing left to lose, you repair it piece by piece and prepare to face the dangers that lie beyond the ruins.
