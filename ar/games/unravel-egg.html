إزاي تلعب

The Unravel Eggs is an HTML5 logic puzzle game with a minimalistic clear design. The goal of the game is to unravel the eggs that are connected by lines of fate. The game has 50 levels. To pass the level you need to unravel the eggs. You need to grab the eggs and move them so that they turn green. In this case, the lines should not intersect and also turn green.
