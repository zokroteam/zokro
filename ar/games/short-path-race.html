إزاي تلعب

Race forward, think fast, and build your own path to victory! Sprint along a narrow track surrounded by water while collecting tiles scattered across the course. Use them wisely to create shortcuts, cross gaps, and outsmart the competition but be careful. Spending all your tiles too early could leave you stranded when the final platforms demand perfect planning. Balance speed, strategy, and timing as every decision shapes your route to the finish line. Easy to start but challenging to master, this fast-paced runner tests both reflexes and smart resource management. Will you rush ahead or save enough tiles to survive the final stretch?
