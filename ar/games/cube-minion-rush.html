إزاي تلعب

Color Human is a fun, fast-paced game where you break as many blocks as possible. Merge humans of different colors to grow your crowd and become stronger. Inspired by snake-style gameplay, you move, collect, and destroy obstacles in your path. The game is easy to play but hard to master. As your crowd gets bigger, you can smash more blocks and score higher. Timing and smart movement are key to success. Enjoy an exciting mix of action and puzzle as you build the biggest crowd and clear your way forward.
