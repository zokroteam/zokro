إزاي تلعب

'Lovely Mania' is a fast paced Heart matching game. Send your heart towards another heart with same color, It will collect all the hearts in the row. The row of hearts keep coming down to chase your heart so be fast to collect every row. Your hearts keeps changing colors so keep checking before sending it towards another heart. You can play a round until the other hearts don’t chase you.
