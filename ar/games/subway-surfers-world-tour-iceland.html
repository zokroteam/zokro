إزاي تلعب

Hot countries got tired of the surfer racer and he decided to visit places where it is cool again and went straight to Iceland. You don't have to travel huge distances on airplanes, just enter the Subway Surfers Iceland game and you are already at the start. The policeman is at the ready and the driver too. He will run the first hundred meters as in training, so that you can remember what and how to control the guy. Further, it all depends on your agility and skill. In addition to traditional obstacles in the form of special barriers, you need to control the movement of trains. The hero can run with his legs or race along the rails on the stage in Subway Surfers Iceland.
