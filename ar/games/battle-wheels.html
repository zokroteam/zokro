إزاي تلعب

Battle Wheels is an explosive vehicle combat arcade where two armored cars smash into each other until one driver loses his head &amp;mdash; literally! Win 3 rounds to claim victory and climb through five leagues: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond and Obsidian. Earn coins from every win and spend them in the garage: buy faster cars, tougher wheels, and boost your Damage and Health stats. Customize your fighter with different heads, eyes, mouths and colors to build your perfect avatar. Play solo against 1000 unique AI opponents, or grab a friend for chaotic 2 Player local battles &amp;mdash; red vs blue, last head standing wins. Simple to pick up, hard to put down.
