إزاي تلعب

Have you ever seen such a Cute Monster like him? His name is Bobo and he eats a lot. He loves to eat desserts like pastries, donuts, cakes, and ice cream. We put the attractive bakery theme to give you the feel of the game. In this game, you need to feed the hungry bobo by tapping on the right dessert at the right time. After reaching a point the game gets harder, so you need to focus more. Try to tap fast to increase the time. The faster you tap much time you get.&lt;/br&gt;&lt;/br&gt; This game is full of fun even when the game is over you will also love the game over the sound. If you are good at tapping then just try to reach 300 scores. So what are you waiting for? Just &quot;Start Feeding Bobo&quot;.
