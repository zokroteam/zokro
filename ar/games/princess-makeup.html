Princess Makeup is a very cute make up game. There are 3 princesses who need your help to attend a party. Each of them has many problems. Come and help them get into shape and look good for the party. One princess needs a face spa, one princess needs a haircut and one princess needs to get her nails done. After you solve their problems, give them nice makeup and dress them up with fashionable outfits and accessories. Want to know more about this game? Come and enjoy it!
