Sand Route Puzzle is a relaxing yet brain-teasing puzzle game where colorful sand rains down and you must catch it in the right containers. Plan your moves, match the colors, and clear every pattern to win! With one-tap controls and satisfying sand physics, its the perfect game for a quick play or a long unwind session. Use special boosters like Vacuum, Jumble, and Extra Cart to overcome tricky levels. Collect daily rewards and unlock endless puzzles easy to start, rewarding to master!
