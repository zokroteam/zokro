Police Bus Simulation puts you behind the wheel of a heavy-duty police bus on a mission to catch criminals on the run. This fast-paced arcade chaser blends pursuit action with vehicle handling. Drive through city streets in both day and night themed levels, chase down thief cars, and ram them to make an arrest. What makes it unique? Youre not in a small police car &amp;mdash; you control a massive bus that requires smart positioning and timing to trap speeding vehicles. Each level brings faster and more aggressive criminals.
