Giant Rush Online is undoubtedly the most interesting new arcade game this year, with addictive gameplay and 3D art style. In this game, you can not only experience the most thrilling parkour game but also play battle games. Your task is to collect different kinds of fruits on the platform. Each time you collect a kind of fruit, your color will change according to the fruit. Take care to avoid different kinds of other fruits. It is worth noting that you can not only run on the platform. , You can also play against other people.
