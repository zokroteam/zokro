Edujoy presents Doctor Pet Vet for children! This animal care game is a really entertaining educational game for children up to 8 years old with which you will learn to take care of your favorite animals. OBJECTIVE The objective is to take the veterinarian's role and to quickly heal the pets of your office from any disease of problem they may have. The game is easy: you have to follow the indicated steps and use the suitable tools. The pets can have a cold, fever, allergy, earache, sore throat or stomach ache. You will also learn to heal wounds and scratches, broken nails and even to remove splinters, fleas and ticks from them. Take care of domestic, farm and of zoo animals and assist them in their recovery to turn into the best vet of all!
