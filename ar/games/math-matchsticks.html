إزاي تلعب

Math Matchsticks is a reasoning puzzle game with simple rules that are fun and challenging for players of all ages. Players need to change numbers or symbols by moving matchsticks according to a given mathematical problem to make the equation work. The game has a total of 100 levels, as the level increases, the difficulty gradually increases, requiring more thinking and patience of the player. This reasoning and calculation game can exercise the players intellectual and mathematical skills.
