A new doll styling app where you pick hairstyles, facial features, makeup, apparel and accessories for a Japanese young chick and a guy on their romantic go-out. Go glam / classy for a prom queen outfit, and maybe make the guy look like a quarterback of the college baseball team. Be the fashion-, hairstyle- and looks-designer for the young lovers. Fill first date with romance and happiness, and the girl would write a cute love poem in her romantic diary about her crush tonight.
