Flower Magnet is a relaxing and enchanting puzzle game where you guide a magical magnet to collect beautiful blossoms. Use strategy and timing to gather every bloom and complete each level with grace. With soothing music, elegant visuals, and increasingly clever puzzles, Flower Magnet is perfect for players seeking calm yet challenging gameplay.
