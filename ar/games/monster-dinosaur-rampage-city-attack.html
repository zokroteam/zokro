إزاي تلعب

Monster Dinosaur Rampage : City Attack brings an adventurous journey in the prehistoric life of dinosaurs. You have to play the role of deadly dinosaurs and attack the city as rampage game. Unlike other rampage games, it would be a journey to the jurassic world. Become a deadly predator of the prehistoric era and start rampage missions in modern world. The evil scientists have tried to perform experiments on some dinosaur species. You play the role of those dinosaurs under experiment and destroy the city for revenge. The military forces will attack you with bullets, dodge their attacks and destroy them.
