Run, collect, and survive in Candle Run, a fun 3D endless runner adventure! Control a burning candle as it races through exciting paths filled with obstacles and challenges. Your goal is to collect candle parts along the way to keep the candle from melting completely. Stay alert and react quickly as every run is different thanks to randomly generated levels. Avoid obstacles, gather as many candle pieces as possible, and keep your flame burning for as long as you can. Test your reflexes, improve your skills, and chase your highest score in this fast-paced hyper-casual game!
