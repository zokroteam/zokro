إزاي تلعب

Roll Ball: Hole Puzzle is a fun 3D ball rolling game! Guide your ball across floating platforms in the sky and roll it into the hole to finish each level. Every level is different: dodge sliding walls, spinning bars and bombs, cross narrow bridges, slide over ice, bounce on spring pads, fight the wind, race over crumbling floors and press buttons to open gates. New traps unlock as you progress, so every few levels bring a fresh challenge. Collect gems and 3 stars on every level, unlock cool ball skins in the shop, use checkpoints and keep your 3 lives safe. Simple one-finger controls, endless levels and smooth physics - how far can you roll?
