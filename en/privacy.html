🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Short version: playing needs no account. Accounts are optional and only store what is needed to run the site.

1. Playing without an account

All games run in your browser. Streaks, votes and best scores are stored on your own device (localStorage) and never leave it unless you create an account.

2. If you create an account (optional)

We store via our auth provider (Supabase): your email, display name, avatar (emoji or a photo you upload) and game scores. Login providers (Google, Facebook, Discord) share your basic profile (name, email, avatar) with us when you choose them. We never see your passwords for those providers.

3. Cookies & ads

We use minimal cookies to keep you logged in and remember your language. When ads are enabled (Google AdSense, after approval), Google and partners may use cookies to show relevant ads. You can block cookies in your browser — games still work.

4. Third parties

  • Supabase — accounts & scores database.
  • Google / Facebook / Discord — only if you log in with them.
  • CDNs — flag images, fonts and game libraries (no tracking by us).
  • GameDistribution / Famobi (future licensed games) — their own terms apply inside their games.

5. Children

Zokro games are family-friendly and need no account. Children should only create accounts with a parent's permission.

6. Your rights

Ask for a copy or deletion of your data anytime at zokro.team@gmail.com. Deleting your account removes your profile and scores.

7. Changes

We update this page when something changes. Continued use of Zokro means you accept the current version.
