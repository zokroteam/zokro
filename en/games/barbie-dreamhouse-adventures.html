How to play

Create your very own Barbie DreamHouse experience! Join us for a bunch of fun activities: baking, cooking, dancing, makeovers, home design, fashion, nail salon, hair salon, mini games or epic pool parties! Explore Malibu with my pink convertible or dress me and my friends up in fashion-forward looks to get picture ready! Follow us on exciting adventures in the DreamHouse where anything is possible! LET'S MOVE IN Are you into home design makeovers? Help me design every room with wonderful wallpapers and dazzling decorations. Make it your own Dreamhouse!
