How to play

Helix Rush is a fast-paced 3D helix jump arcade game! Rotate the spiral tower and guide the bouncing ball down through the gaps between platforms. Smash through colorful levels, but watch out for the red danger zones - one wrong bounce and it is game over! Skip 3 or more platforms in a single fall to enter Fever Mode and crush everything in your path. Collect coins on every run and unlock 12 unique ball skins in the shop. Climb through endless procedurally generated levels with steadily increasing difficulty, chase your high score and become the Helix master! Features: Endless level progression with rising difficulty - Fever Mode after long free falls - 12 unlockable ball skins - Coin rewards and shop - Quick revive to continue your run - One-finger controls, instant fun! Perfect hypercasual time killer for kids and adults.
