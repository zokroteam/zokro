How to play

In Pokey Balls, you have to throw the ball up in the most nervous way to get the ball to the highest level. The higher you throw the ball, the more points you earn. When the ball starts to fall, fix the ball in a suitable place and throw the ball again. When you reach the top, a reward awaits you. Play Pokey Balls game with +50 different levels without getting bored.
