How to play

Focus your hawk-eyes in this hilarious test of your attention, which is best described as an animated version of the classical Odd One Out puzzle. Find the fool! Forget Wally or Waldo. Can you spot Walter the Wacky Walker among hundreds of runners?! Beware, he prefers to be left alone … KEY GAME FEATURES This game is made to work equally well with MOUSE and TOUCH. It also works perfectly on all screen sizes and all proportions from LANDSCAPE 3:1 to PORTRAIT 1:3. ENDLESS numbers of ultra short levels. Early levels have an UNIQUE level-completed animation. Later levels quickly become extremely difficult. RANKING and LEADERBOARD stimulate repeated plays to perfect execution of early levels. Average time from start to game over = 15-60 secs.
