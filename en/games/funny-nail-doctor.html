How to play

Funny Nail Doctor is a cute mouse skill game where you will play as a doctor. A young lady visited your clinic. She has problems with her nails on her hands and feet. It's giving her an uncomfortable feeling and she wants it to get healed as soon as possible. So as her doctor, you need to do procedures that will make her wounds heal. Clean it and apply the needed medicines for her nails. After you successfully cure her nails, you can now do some nail makeover for her so she will feel a lot better.
