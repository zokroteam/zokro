How to play

The objects in Ball Color3D are composed of various geometric shapes, including common spheres, cubes, and rhomboids, as well as various irregular 3D shapes. Adjust the angle by touching the screen, shoot the small ball to the part corresponding to the 3D shape, and color the small ball around the shooting target. Gorgeous colors and unlimited levels will show you the beauty of the game!
