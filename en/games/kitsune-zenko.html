Kitsune is the Japanese word for fox and the Zenko mean good, literally good foxes are benevolent. But not so easily, the enemy has been waiting for you. How to play &gt; Use in game pad to move your player, use jump button for jumping. &gt; Avoid enemies, kill em if possible.
