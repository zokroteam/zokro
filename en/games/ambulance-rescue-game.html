How to play

Ambulance Rescue Game puts you in the role of an emergency rescue driver, where every second counts. Respond to emergency calls, race through city streets and challenging roads, rescue injured patients, and transport them safely to the hospital. Avoid traffic, navigate difficult routes, and complete life-saving missions against the clock. Earn rewards, unlock new ambulances, and take on more demanding rescue operations as your skills improve. With realistic driving physics, detailed 3D environments, and exciting gameplay, Ambulance Rescue Game delivers an action-packed rescue simulation experience.
