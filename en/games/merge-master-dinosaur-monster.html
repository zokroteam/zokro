How to play

Merge Dragon Master Battle 3D is a cool real-time strategy game for everyone. You need to think fast to beat all the enemies by combing your dragons on your battlefield. Combine quickly and actively attack enemies to gain control of enemy positions and possess greater power than the opponent. At the same time, use your strategy and tactics to win the battle. Grow your dragons from simple monsters to terrific monsters when you merge them. Use your finger to draw the right combination and evolve your monsters. The higher the level of the monster dragon, the stronger the attack and defense. Remember, if you don't merge them fast enough, your enemy monster dragons will beat you in the final round fight. Merge your army as fast as you can because the monsters will get stronger after each level. Unlock new stronger dragons. Only 1% of players can unlock all to win.
