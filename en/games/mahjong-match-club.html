🧩 Mahjong Match Club

★★★★★ 4.5 • 🎮 puzzle • 📱 Mobile friendly

🏆 Top players

How to play

Choose two matching mahjongs and remove them. Following removal, a moveable green space will emerge. Mahjong pieces should be moved until there are no more obstacles in either the horizontal or vertical directions. It faces another mahjong that is the same. Release your grip, and they will naturally vanish. Youll advance to the next level and receive points. The game puts strategy and vision to the test. Are you able to finish the challenge?
