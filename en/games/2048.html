How to play

Use arrow keys or swipe to slide all tiles. Matching numbers merge into their sum. Reach the 2048 tile to win — the game continues after that if you dare.

The board fills up fast. Keep your biggest tile in one corner and never trap yourself.

Tips to win

  • Keep the largest tile in a corner
  • Plan two moves ahead
  • Don't rush — every slide counts
