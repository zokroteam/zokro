How to play

Looking for an online version of classic Card Matching games? Play a refreshed version of this beloved game here! Win to earn coins and use coins for powerups. Powerups will help you for online matching duels, where you must go against an opponent to match as many cards as possible in one board. As you progress online youll get trophies to advance to new and better leagues, awarding more coins and harder opponents. If youre more of a casual player, you can still play a regular level mode, where you have to find pairs of cards in a bigger and bigger board with a set of lives to do it.You lose a life not finding a pair.
