How to play

Spatial Inverse is an innovative, free-to-play physics puzzle game where you manipulate gravity to guide glowing marbles to their goals. Designed for players who love logic games, brain teasers, and physics simulations, this HTML5 browser game offers a seamless, download-free experience. Set across 15 meticulously designed levels in 3 unique sci-fi worlds, every stage forces you to think differently about direction, momentum, and space. In a universe where ceilings become floors and walls become slides, you must strategically place gadgets like blocks, bounce pads, and portals in the grid, then hit Play and watch gravity.
