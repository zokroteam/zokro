How to play

Funny Pet Haircut is a funny simulation game. Be the owner of your pet salon and groom all the cute customers until they're squeaky clean and gorgeous! Brush them, de-flea them, wash them and shampoo them before changing their colors, hairstyles, make-up, and accessories. Go ahead and give Funny Pet Haircut a try!
