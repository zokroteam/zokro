How to play

Have you ever wanted to just walk around and shoot stuff? Now you can! In this simulation game you are at the firing range and you will start with a Pistol. You can move freely around the map and there are targets you can shoot at. Pick up new cool weapons on the wall like a Shotgun, M16, Sniper and Grenade Launcher. Try them out and have fun!
