How to play

You start with 100 water. Heat rises every 10 seconds and evaporates your water. Use 3 buttons: Drink (+8 water), Shade (-heat, costs water), Rest (+score but +heat). Survive the full 60 seconds to win. Same challenge for everyone today - compare scores with friends.

Tips to win

  • Don't drink early - save score for the last 20 hot seconds.
  • Use Shade right before heat crosses 50°.
  • Rest only when water is above 60.
