How to play

Prepare for the battle of Ragdoll Heroes. Choose your hero and start the battle right away. At the end of each battle, you will earn mines and diamonds. You can produce new weapons with the mines you earn. Or you can strengthen your weapon. You can get stronger with different characters. You can also gain an advantage in battle by improving your character. Prepare to proclaim your heroism with multiplayer mode, story mode, survival mode and training mode!
