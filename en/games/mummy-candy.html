How to play

Mummy Candy is a fun physics-based cut-the-rope puzzle game set in ancient Egypt. Help a hungry little mummy get the tasty candies by cutting ropes at the right moment so the treats fall into its mouth. Guide candies past obstacles, avoid rocks and explosives, and collect bonus stars and treasure. Complete each levels targets, earn rewards and unlock more challenging puzzles with new mechanics and power-ups. With colorful cartoon graphics, cute characters and satisfying physics, its easy to learn and endlessly fun on both mobile and desktop. Can you feed the mummy all the candy?
