How to play

Draw Pen Rush is a fun and exciting parkour game. Players control a robot to compete in parkour races, avoiding obstacles and collecting various parts to enhance their abilities and turn into big robots. The game is played in portrait mode, allowing players to see further down the track and prepare to avoid traps. Help the robot defeat the ultimate brain.
