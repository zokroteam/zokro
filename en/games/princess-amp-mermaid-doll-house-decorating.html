How to play

Create your dream houses in a fun and entertaining decorating game! What do you say about decorating underwater houses and castles? Join our 8 princesses and 8 mermaids in one of the prettiest decorating games and challenge your inner interior designer. Show off your decorating skills in any circumstances! Castles and underwater houses are waiting for you! First, choose between the 2 worlds, and then just pick a house. Match and mix various types of furniture and decorations: sofas, beds, cabinets, rugs, shelves and many more. Transform each room into a magical place to live. You can go for a pink concept for the bedroom, or pick a green bed and a purple wardrobe. Even if they are princesses or mermaids, our girls love to cook. Add modern appliances in the kitchen and choose some warm colors for the walls and the floor. Don't forget to always add a final touch with ornaments and decorations. Shells for mermaids and unicorns for the princesses! Create the most amazing houses in each world. There are lots of furniture options available for every room, explore all of them. You'll never get bored playing this decorating game!
