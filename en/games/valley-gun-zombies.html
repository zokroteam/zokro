How to play

In the valley there were zombies, it's time for you to uncover a sniper rifle and begin cleaning the territory of stray ghouls. Try to take the monster on sight when it is far away. The red dot of the laser sight will help you not to miss. Do not allow the zombies to approach, you will not have time to react as he jumps up and stabs sharp teeth in the neck. Do not be deceived by the slowness of zombies, they attack instantly.
