How to play

Snake Eats Apple is a fun online game where you control a snake and try to eat as many apples as possible. The objective of the game is to grow your snake by eating the apples, but be careful not to run into the walls or your own tail! The game gets more challenging as your snake grows longer, so make sure to plan your moves carefully. So, stay focused and try to beat your high score with each playthrough! Enjoy playing
