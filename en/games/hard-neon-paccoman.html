How to play

Relive the legendary arcade spirit with a futuristic twist! PACCOMAN brings classic maze-runner mechanics into a stunning Cyber-Neon aesthetic. Every level is a new challenge. Thanks to our procedural generation engine, no two mazes are ever the same. As you progress, the speed intensifies and the spectral hunters become more aggressive. Can you keep your cool under the glowing lights? Experience fluid controls designed for both Desktop and Mobile gamers. Whether you are using a keyboard or touch gestures, your reflexes are the only thing standing between a high score and Game Over.
