How to play

Welcome to the explosive world of Boom Stick Bazooka 2, a free online game to destroy platforms and enemy stickmen with your customizable bazooka. Aim and shoot to eliminate the towers where the black stickmen are, some of which if you delay will shoot you trying to eliminate your 3 lives. You will have 3 opportunities to aim and shoot in each level, you can repeat at any time or buy more ammunition. To eliminate enemies faster, try to destroy the barrels so that they explode nearby and destroy the towers.
