How to play

Choose the best gate, pass through it, and bring alphabet stickman warriors together to clash with the opposing crowd. In Alphabet Count Rush, you start alone but quickly grow your letter crowd by choosing the right paths and counting smartly. Become the crowd master and lead your alphabet army through the crowded city till the end of this epic race. Crash obstacles, avoid traps, and beat rival letter groups on your way. Collect coins, unlock upgrades, and increase your power. Defeat the final alphabet boss in an intense battle and take control of the arena. Looking for fun, thrill, and fast-paced action? This 3D running game is just for you.
