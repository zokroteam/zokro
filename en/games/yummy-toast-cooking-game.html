How to play

Yummy Toast is a cooking game where you learn how to make delicious toast by tapping the correct materials and ingredients you need. Start by cleaning the stove and the frying pan, then prepare all the ingredients required for the recipes. Make the tastiest toast and serve it to your customers with the help of your teddy bear friend. Are you ready to become the best toastmaster in town and unlock all the achievements? Enjoy!
