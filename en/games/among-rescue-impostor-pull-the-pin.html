How to play

Among Rescue is a challenging puzzle (pull the pin) game in which you need to collect all the treasures, save the hero and defeat the impostors. Pull the pin in order to destroy your enemies impostors. A game from the &quot;Pull the Pin&quot; puzzle series» If the Hero makes a wrong move, he will die. Distinguish yourself in the game with the sophistication of thinking to cope with the logical tasks of &quot;Rescue&quot;. The spacecraft is divided by pins into compartments. One contains lava, the other contains water. You need to remove the pins in the game so as to fill the fire and get the jewels for the hero. Gameplay: Easy and fun gameplay; Lots of challenging puzzles and unique levels; Learn how to pull out a pin; Use your brain to save the hero; No time and life are limited, enjoy yourself. * This application is entertainment and does not claim copyright in any way.
