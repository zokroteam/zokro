Instead, being a queen is an empowered role where you rule a whole nation, millions of people. It's important to look appropriately and seriously for such a big task. Although there is a dress code (with a high weight of tradition) for most such occasions, there is still room for a talented fashion designer to come up with new imaginative outfits.
