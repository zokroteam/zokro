How to play

Arietta and her friend and family are going on vacation at a camping this summer! Help these crazy campers with everything for the camping. The campers needs to pack their camping stuff first! Help with shopping camping stuff like a tent and pack everything for a crazy holiday! These kids do like a real survival, search for wood and a stone to make a hammer and wood for your tent and a campfire or a bonfire. Clean up your camping spot and set up the tent. When the tent is up, they can start with their crazy camping summer days! Fishing, eating, bonfires, playing and having fun on the camping and they will have the best vacation ever! After packing these friends can go on their camping trip in the summer! Have fun with these kids on their campground.
