How to play

Pet Vet Care lets you play doctor to a wide variety of fluffy lovable animals Love animals? Have the best playtime fun with Pet Vet Care! Dogs, cats, rabbits, hamsters and even pandas – all these cute creatures need your help! Take on the role of a kind and loving vet, and nurse a wide variety of animals back to health. Play with and train them, ready to find their forever homes. For the young at heart, Pet Vet Care lets you play doctor to a wide variety of lovable animals, as they feed, wash and have hours of fun with dogs, cats, pandas and more! Meet the adorable, squishy-nosed pug. Pet the two fluffy cats. Go barking mad with a German Shepherd and befriend the woolly sheep. Get to know the cute, cuddly rabbit and the playful pony, or have a long chat to the parrot. And there are even more animals to keep you entertained, including a mischievous little squirrel, a happy hamster, a tiny turtle, a dapper duck, a little pink piggy, a furry panda and a prickly hedgehog! Discover a whole new world of fun! Feed them! Pet them! Take them to the store! Everything you need to keep these fluffy critters happy is right there, with a whole town at your fingertips, where you can find all the supplies you need to make sure they always stay healthy.
