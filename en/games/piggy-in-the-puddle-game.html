How to play

A pig and a bathtub? It does not mix well... Add mud in the bathtub... Immediately, things are different! Pigs love to wade in the mud, even if it's in a bathtub. Help our friend to reach the bathtub on each level by making it roll and fall. You can change its shape: when it is round, it rolls and can thus collect food at the same time.
