How to play

In Finger Heart: Monster Refill, your task is to creatively combine your finger with cute monsters to craft the perfect heart shape. No special skills or expertise are required; all you need is your imagination, intuition, and a sprinkle of luck. The gameplay ranges from easy to challenging, occasionally taking a whimsical turn, but it consistently delivers enjoyment and gratification. Heres how to play: choose the correct shape or draw the right line, then drag the shape into the designated space and adjust the monster to fit. Keep an eye on the countdown timer.
