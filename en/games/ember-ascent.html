How to play

Ember Ascent is a one-tap sky-climbing arcade game. Launch your dragon skyward and tap to boost -- the sky keeps changing the higher you climb, from the Ember Foothills and Storm Peaks to the Frozen Summit, the Void, and beyond. Dodge crows, lightning bats, storm clouds, frost wisps and void wraiths, then face a unique Guardian boss at each zones gate. Collect embers to unlock dragon skins, permanent upgrades, and fill out the collection codex across three difficulty modes.
