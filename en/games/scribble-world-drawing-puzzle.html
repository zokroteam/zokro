How to play

Embark on a delightful and captivating journey of creativity and problem-solving in Scribble World: Drawing Puzzle. In this third game in our beloved series, you must interact with physics-based elements like seesaws, trampolines and even bubbles to assist Scribball in a unique way: by drawing lines with a pencil. You can create the path that Scribbal needs to traverse each challenge. Your creativity is your tool and Scribball depends on you to find a way out.
