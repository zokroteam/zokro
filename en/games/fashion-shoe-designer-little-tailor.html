How to play

If you love to design your shoes then this game made for you people. In this advance era, everyone loves fashion and new designs in footwear. If you are keen to become a fabulous shoe designer then this game let you design your shoe, as you want. You can uniquely design your sandals, pumps and high heels shoes. Give your dreams a real look to show other that how talented shoe designer you are. You will fall in love with this sensational fashion designer game. The fabulous feature of this game is you have a vast variety of shoe making material and you can choose your desired shoe maker item and color. Do you want pair of sandals, pumps or a winter shoes collection? There is everything you want; you just have to choose your desired shoe designs in this fashion salon. Work like an artistic shoe maker in the high heels shoe maker factory. First part of this game is to choose leather material, color, and customize your shoes design. After selection, it is time to shoe lacing. Application of beautiful jewelry diamonds and beads will give an outstanding look to your shoe design. So design it to show your designing skills in the shoe maker factory and prove that you are a hero of this fashion world and shoe carnival. You can use designs as much as you want to design your high heels shoes. Just use different concepts and ideas to design your high heel shoes. After creating shoe design, it is time to wrap your high heels shoes in the shoe box. Wrap your tremendous shoe collection carefully in the shoe box and hand over it to the customer. At the end receive your cash and your task will be finish. Be a successful shoe designer by playing in this high heel shoe making factory.
