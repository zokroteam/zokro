How to play

Heroes Unite: Idle Clicker is a free idle clicker game where you recruit powerful superheroes, defeat dangerous villains, and create the strongest hero team ever! Collect and upgrade heroes inspired by iconic comic-book legends, each with unique abilities and increasing power. Tap to deal damage, defeat waves of enemies, take down giant bosses, and conquer new city districts while earning gold, diamonds, and other valuable rewards. Upgrade your heroes, unlock powerful skills, and use the PRESTIGE system to gain permanent bonuses that make every new run even stronger. Even when youre offline, your heroes continue earning rewards.
