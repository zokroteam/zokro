How to play

Going Up Rooftop is a 3D parkour adventure across the rooftops of a sprawling city. Climb walls, leap between ledges, crawl through gaps, slide, roll and ride ziplines as you make your way higher and higher above the streets. Ladders, poles and scaffolding all become part of the route &amp;mdash; the only rule is to keep moving up. Three ways to play: follow the Story, take on pure Parkour runs, or test yourself in Climb and Jump mode. Watch your stamina, hit the checkpoints and grab coins along the way to unlock new outfits for your character. Weather changes as you climb, with rain, snow and wind making the higher floors trickier.
