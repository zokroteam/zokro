How to play

Bubble shooters no longer offer banal multi-colored bubbles as game elements. Whom you will not meet on the playing field in the form of round bubbles: sweets, monsters, and the game Bubble Animal Saga offers you round faces of pets and inhabitants of rural yards. If you look closely, you will see the familiar faces of puppies, piglets, cockerels, and even some angry birds hid among them. Your task is to knock them down by firing bubbles. By collecting a group of three or more of the same, you will make them fall down in Bubble Animal Saga.
