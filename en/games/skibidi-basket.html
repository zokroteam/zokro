How to play

Skibidi Toilet Basketball is a simple but very Addictive game which base on realistic physics! Tap and slide the Skibidi Toilet ball, control its angle and power, then shoot towards the hoop. The perfect effect and physic effect will help you see more clearly. The game is an endless arcade game, then shoot towards the hoop to win the levels; as the difficulty could increases, there are four different random movements for the hoop. You have just 3 shoots to complete each level! Are you able to try to sign the best record in the leaderboard? HOW TO PLAY: 1. Touch the screen! 2. Drag the ball to change the power and angle! 3. Release to shoot!
