How to play

Help small defenseless sheep find their way back home. They are completely lost among all these roads and intersections. But they are not alone on their journey... Wolves are waiting to devour them! Protect all the sheep by managing their moves. If a sheep is devoured, the game is over. The sheep community thanks you for your help.
