How to play

Are You Ready To Take Action on a Great Prison Escape Plan to prove that you are not what they think you are.. You must strategies and slip trough high security facility by identifying their loop holes.. You are sentenced 15 years imprisonment in a worst prison to be in. Break out &amp; escape the bars to prove you are innocence and to gain your prime right to be free in this top secret survival prison escape plan game. The Game Features the maximum security prison to keep inmates in for life. So you are going to deal with the real challenge here. The Prison warden has the maximum security in place. Escaping from the Prison guards only seems to be a dream. But In order to gain you reputation and freedom you must make and deploy a master plan and break out of this hell like place and to do something good with your life rather than rotting in this hell. The Maximum security consists of a fully camera rigged, most secured prison crawling with prison guards.. So You must beat the system here being at your best. You shall have to disable cameras systems, disable metal detectors. You shall have to sneak pass laser beam security units and will have to access and unlock many doors and many sections of the Prison using valid key-cards. You shall have to pave your way through these suicide like missions with concentration and focus at your current object one at a time.
