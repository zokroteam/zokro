How to play

He Likes the Darkness is a cool platform jumping game . In this delightful title you control a cute little black monster who as you would guess, loves the darkness! Use your keyboard arrow keys to move the monster left and right, and to make him jump. You must collect all the gold stars and coins on each level before processing to the next one. The gameplay starts of simple and you just have to jump across to various different platforms. As you progress through the different worlds however, the challenges become increasingly complex. Some levels contain moving platforms and you must time your jumps perfectly! With 7 different worlds (each world contains 8 individual levels), this platform game will keep you entertained for hours!
