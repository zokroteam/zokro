How to play

Sery Actress Dolly Makeup is a fun and exciting makeup game where you get to be a professional makeup artist and work with the beautiful and talented actress Dolly. In this game, your objective is to help Dolly achieve her desired look for her next big event or performance. Youll have access to a wide variety of makeup products, including foundations, eyeshadows, lipsticks, and more, and youll need to choose the right colors and shades to enhance Dollys natural beauty. Youll also have the opportunity to experiment with different hairstyles and accessories to complete her overall look. As you progress through the game, youll unlock new levels and challenges that will put your makeup skills to the test. Youll need to be creative and strategic in your makeup choices to ensure that Dolly looks her absolute best.
