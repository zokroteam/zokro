★★★★★ 4.5 • 🎮 kids • 📱 Mobile friendly

🏆 Top players

How to play

Our hero will go to a special site and there will stand on the platform. From different sides will appear a different kind of plate, which will move at different speeds. You will have to wait until the platform comes up to our hero and click on the screen. The hero will jump and jump on the block. For this you will get points. If you do not have time to do this, then your hero will hit the block and it will fall off the platform.
