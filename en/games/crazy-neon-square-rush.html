How to play

Crazy Neon Square Rush is a fast-paced hyper-casual arcade game where reflexes and focus are everything. Control a glowing neon square as it constantly moves upward through an endless stream of colorful obstacles. The longer you survive, the faster and more intense the game becomes. Earn score automatically by staying alive, dodge neon blocks, and push your limits to beat your best score. With smooth controls, vibrant neon visuals, dynamic difficulty scaling, and a clean modern interface, Crazy Neon Square Rush delivers a satisfying and addictive arcade experience. Easy to learn, hard to master, and perfect for short or long play sessions.
