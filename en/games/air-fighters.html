How to play

Prepare for missions and take up the challenge of world supremacy now. Destroy your targets, whether land, sea or air. Face waves of enemy planes in air battles. Thousands of new missions are available every day: it's an adventure without limits! Realistic world maps and navigation. Over 500 real airports, 1,107 airstrips, and real-time weather conditions. AN IMMERSIVE GAMEPLAY Train and prepare to fight. Take on the challenge of world supremacy, where you will have to complete the best online missions in the world. Become a pilot in aerial combat, facing ever more powerful enemy planes: 6 game modes that will make you an elite pilot. Unlock ever more powerful weaponry for each plane. AIRCRAFT CARRIER - NAVAL OPERATIONS Use aircraft carriers or airports as bases for your missions and expand your reach. CREATE YOUR MISSIONS Choose a location in the world and create your tactical missions using a wide range of different air / land / sea targets. Upload your best missions for other players to rate. AN UNPRECEDENTED REALISM 569 of the world's largest airports have been faithfully reproduced, with their surroundings and airstrip. The planes you will fly are perfectly realistic whether it is for their performance, their characteristics or their fuel tank: try vertical take-offs, variable-geometry wings, and board the cockpits of the best planes in the world. The flight engine allows you to fly around the world, but watch your fuel reserves, and don't forget to request an in-flight refueling before it's too late.
