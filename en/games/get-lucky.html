How to play

How does the girl win her love in the swimming pool? Get Lucky is actually not a puzzle game but a collecting vertical-version arcade game with a 3D girl and colorful coat hangers. Your objective is to charge the left bar up on the red area at each level by means of collecting coat hangers while avoiding red blankets. From you start collecting coat hangers, you are able to change and take off your current clothes as the girl. Keep calm and collect as many coat hangers as you can while avoiding red blankets!
