How to play

Crazy Car Drive is the most addictive endless car driving game. This game will let you be a great car driver. The goal of this highway racer game is to drive your car and survive against upcoming crazy traffic rush and enemies, don't let them crash you or you will lose. Sounds easy? But, would you be able to drive speeding car, survive and avoid hitting your enemies? How to Play : It has simple, yet challenging game play. You need to use your phone’s accelerometer to control the car Avoid Crashing the Enemy Cars Avoid hitting other vehicles on the road, you lose health Collect as much of fuel as you can, it increases your health Be alert while you drive. Game becomes challenging with time, driving becomes hard
