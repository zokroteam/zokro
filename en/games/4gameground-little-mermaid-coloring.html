How to play

Coloring book &quot;Little Mermaid&quot; is a cool coloring game for children and adults. Play the Little Mermaid Coloring Book online for free. This game can be played on all devices. First you choose which of the eight pages you want to color. At the bottom of the game screen you can find the colors that were available to you, and you just click on the desired color, and to color it, click and hold them, moving the mouse the way you want. From under the page, you can also choose an eraser if you need one, and also choose how big the point from which you will color will be. Now that you know all this, start the game immediately and don't look back for a second!
