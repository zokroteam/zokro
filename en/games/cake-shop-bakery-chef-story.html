How to play

This is exciting news for all baking game enthusiasts and aspiring chefs - your cake shop has opened. Congratulations! First of all, you need to know your daily work: 1. Clean the cake shop and make it clean. 2. Tidy up and repair the little dolls on the doll rack, so that they are neat and new. 3. Receive customers and remember the shape and color of the cake they want. 4. Start mixing. Pour water, butter, eggs and other ingredients into a bowl proportionally and mix well. 5. Well, yes, we need to pour the mixer into the mold to bake, remember not to bake it. 6. When the cake is baked, we put delicious cream on it and chocolate on it. It looks delicious. By the way, while making and decorating delicious vanilla, chocolate or strawberry cakes for loyal customers, travel around the world!
