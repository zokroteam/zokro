How to play

SpongeBob SquarePants Jump Force is an online HTML5 game , it's playable in browsers such as safari and chrome. There are 30 levels in total, you need to finish all these challenges and help him to become the most outstanding SpongeBob SquarePants. Avoid obstacles on the road and enter the destination to open the next level.
