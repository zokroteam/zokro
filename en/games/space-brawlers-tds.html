How to play

Space Brawlers is a Non-Stop Action Packed Sci-fi Top-Down Shooter! Choose from 4 unique classes, each with tactical counters, health, speed, and 3 cooldown skills. Play as the Mercenary Gunner, a precision fighter who harasses enemies from range, perfect for beginners thanks to his aim-bot ability. The Blitz Scout thrives on lightning speed and supportive powers, but demands accuracy to shred foes. Prefer durability? WHIP out the Stalwart Tank, sacrificing speed for defense and smashing through enemy positions. Or pick Salvus Tech, repairing allies, holding ground with turrets, and breaking stalemates with Hypercharge.
