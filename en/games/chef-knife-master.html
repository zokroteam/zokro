How to play

Those who enjoy spending time in the kitchen or who love cooking! This game is for you. The biggest dream of those who love to cook is to quickly chop the vegetables and fruits they use in cooking. Just like a professional chef. If you cannot do these movements in your kitchen, you can try yourself in the game. Chop the food such as bananas, carrots, peppers, potatoes quickly and try to get the highest score. Don't forget to complete the given tasks. Let's chopping!
