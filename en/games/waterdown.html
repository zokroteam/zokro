How to play

Control a faucet and blast trash into the sink hole using powerful streams of water! Water Down is a satisfying and fast-paced hyper-casual game where you swipe to control the water stream and clear kitchen trash before time runs out. Complete levels by pushing all trash into the hole &amp;mdash; but watch out, every second counts!
