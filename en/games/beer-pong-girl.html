How to play

Hey there! I'm Beergal and I want to challenge all those dudes who think they are better than me, so let's do it : play to beer pong ! The principle is simple: Each their turn the opponents must throw a ping-pong ball into tumblers full of beer from the opponent. Be the first to succeed in throwing the ball into each tumblers otherwise you risk being ridiculed! Let's show them!
