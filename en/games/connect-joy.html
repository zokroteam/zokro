How to play

Welcome to Connect Joy, the exciting Onet puzzle game! Sharpen your mind by connecting 2 identical elements on the board. Tap or click to link two identical tiles with exactly three straight turns to link matching pairs. Explore a vast world of challenging levels and race against the clock to clear the board before time runs out. Good luck!
