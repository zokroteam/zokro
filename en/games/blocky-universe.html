How to play

In Blocky Universe, you set out on an adventure in a world full of monsters! With your trusty bow and your ax, its up to you to defeat hordes of zombies, skeletons and other enemies. To do that, you will have to get strong! Cutting wood will help you become a better and faster archer, while spending coins will help you cut wood faster. By upgrading both, youll become the best combination of a lumberjack and an archer in no time! Can you defeat all the bosses, fix the portal and make it back home?
