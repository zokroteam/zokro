How to play

Cubic Rush is a fast-paced, minimalist 2D endless runner where rhythm and timing are key! You control a vibrant red cube that automatically slides forward on a sleek green path under a sunny yellow sky. Your goal is simple: tap to make the cube jump over the incoming blue obstacles. Each successful jump boosts your score and releases a satisfying pop sound with white bubble particles. But beware! Hitting a blue cube triggers a loss sound and a YOU LOSE screen. The challenge intensifies as your speed increases with every 5 points scored, pushing your reflexes to the limit. Chase a new high score in this endlessly escalating, visually clean, and addictive arcade challenge!
