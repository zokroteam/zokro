How to play

Minecraft Card Matching Puzzle is a fun and relaxing card matching game that tests your memory and attention. Tap on cards to reveal them and match two identical cards to make them disappear. If the cards do not match, they flip back over. Each level contains 16 cards. Once all cards are matched, a new set of 16 cards drops from the top and the challenge continues. The level number indicates how many card sets must be completed. When all sets are successfully matched, the level is completed. With simple controls and Minecraft-style visuals, the game offers an enjoyable experience for players of all ages.
