How to play

One more apple... but will your tail still fit? SNACK &amp;amp; WARP is a colorful puzzle adventure with 67 levels. Eat apples to grow, climb over obstacles and balance across gaps. Lift crates with your head, send them through paired portals, and use buttons to open doors or change platforms. A blade apple splits your worm into two equal halves: switch between them to hold a button, support a crate or jam a saw while the other worm explores. Plan your bites, save room to turn, and collect every apple before reaching the purple exit. Undo mistakes freely and find clever solutions to earn three stars. No timer: take your time and experiment!
