How to play

Flick Shot Soccer is a fun and addictive soccer shooting game where every goal is all about precision. Simply drag to aim and position your shot, then release to kick and send the ball flying into the net. Easy controls make it perfect for anyone to pick up and play, while smart angles and timing are needed to score consistently.
