How to play

The Kickin' it Old School game takes you on an exciting journey with the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles as they're transported back in time due to Kraang's sinister plans. Join the turtles in their quest to defuse the Dimensionizers and save the city before it's too late! Explore the streets and take on the evil creatures in intense battles. With Michelangelo, Donatello, Leonardo, and Raphael taking turns in fighting their long-time foes, use each turtle's strengths and energy wisely to emerge victorious. Get ready for a thrilling ride and show your turtle power!
