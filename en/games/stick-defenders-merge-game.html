How to play

Stick Defenders is an action and merging game where you combine stickman units into stronger ones, so that they can protect your base from phases of enemies. Merge identical gunmen, enhance your offensive abilities, improve your walls, and much more. There are fun side activities like &quot;spin the wheel&quot; where you can get surprising items to help you become stronger and unlock new possibilities. Make sure to use your skills on the oncoming horde of enemies as soon as their cooldown's over, so you don't get overwhelmed by them. How long can you hold down the fort in Stick Defenders?
