How to play

Get ready for a challenging and yet fun game against your friend or artificial intelligence! You are one of two interlocking players. Take control of your player and don’t let the opponent player beat you. In this game, which is a balance game, you can balance or jump by moving to the right and left. The game consists of 11 rounds in total. The player who makes 11 on the leaderboard becomes the winner of the game. The game you need to do is to keep your balance and make sure that your head does not hit the floor. The opposing player wins 1 point when your head touches the floor.
