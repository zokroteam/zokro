How to play

Rolling space ball – Fun 3d game for android Rolling space ball is an addictive 3d game, where you need to roll the ball in a space like environment and cover maximum distance to score more points. As the game goes the colour of the rolling ball on the space changes. Enjoy the sky view as you play the game. Roll the ball as far as possible on space and score as high as possible. How to play • In this game, initially tap the screen to get the ball moving. • Whenever you come across an obstacle, tap the screen to jump. • The points you score will depend on the distance covered.
