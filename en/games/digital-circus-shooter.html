How to play

Digital Circus Shooter is a fast-paced first-person shooting game set inside a colorful and chaotic circus arena. Fight waves of strange digital enemies, earn cash for every defeat, and purchase stronger weapons to survive increasingly difficult rounds. Explore bright toy-like environments, keep moving to avoid enemy attacks, and aim carefully as the arena becomes more dangerous. How long can you survive the madness of the digital circus?
