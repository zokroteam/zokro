How to play

She is princess, but soon or later there will be coronation ceremony and she will become a queen, so she needs to be dressed the way queen looks like. She can freeze everything around and anybody only by touching or looking at it, she got icy heart, that’s why all her dress up is completely frozen. This princess is untouchable for others, but in this dress up game you can make her not only snow princess but also a flame queen. It is up to you to make an ice of a flame in her heart. Big choice of dress will let you to make the right choice.
