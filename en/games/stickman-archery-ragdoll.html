How to play

Stickman Archer is a sleek and athletic black stickman warrior with a simple but powerful silhouette. He has a round head with small glowing white eyes, a slim yet strong body, long flexible limbs, and an aggressive combat stance. He carries a large modern bow with a clearly visible string and a quiver filled with arrows on his back. Simple shoulder armor and an arm guard give him a tougher, more battle-ready appearance without making the character overly detailed. His long limbs are designed to work naturally with ragdoll physics, allowing dramatic reactions when hit, knocked back, launched into the air, or thrown from platforms.
