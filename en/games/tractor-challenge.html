How to play

In the new Tractor Challenge game, you have to conduct field trials of new tractor models that will soon go on sale. Selecting a specific tractor model you will see it in front of you on the screen. The tractor will stand on the road. By pushing the gas pedal, you will ride it on a road with a difficult terrain, gradually gaining speed. You will need to deftly drive a vehicle to overcome many dangerous sections of the road and even make ski jumps. The main thing is not to let the tractor roll over, because then you will fail the test.
