How to play

Gumball Jump Force is an online HTML5 game , it's playable in browsers such as safari and chrome. You can play the game on smartphone and tablet (iPhone, iPad, Samsung, Android devices and Windows Phone). The little Gumball is starting his training. There are 30 levels in total, you need to finish all these challenges and help him to become the most outstanding Gumball . Avoid obstacles on the road and enter the destination to open the next level.
