How to play

Another stickman game in which the player has three game modes. In the first, you need to shoot from a bow at moving and static targets, having a limited number of arrows in stock, in the second mode you will have a duel with another stickman, and in the third you will have to clear a multi-storey building, but using not a bow with arrows, but the most ordinary one gun. In each of the three modes, you have to collect emeralds, this is a currency that can be exchanged for new weapons and skins.
