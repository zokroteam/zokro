How to play

Candy Match Pop is a colorful single-player match-3 puzzle adventure with 80 levels across four chapters. Swap neighboring candies to match three or more, trigger satisfying cascades, and create line blasts, bombs, and rainbow candies. Complete score and collection goals, clear obstacles, collect keys, and protect special candies in move-limited and timed challenges. Use boosters to tackle tricky boards, earn stars and coins, and explore daily rewards, missions, and unlockable themes. Play with a mouse or touch controls on desktop and mobile. Supports English and Chinese, with progress saved in your browser.
