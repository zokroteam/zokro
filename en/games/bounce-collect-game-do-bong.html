How to play

Collect balls in multitudes in this awesome arcade game! Simply drop your balls through a multiplier and watch them grow! Reach the level's end with the level's ball goal or more to complete each level. Do some quick mental math to see which path will net you the most balls! FEATURES It is not tough gameplay, it is really meditative. There is a variety of levels and ways that you need to complete.
