How to play

This is a caring game for girls. Raising one baby is hard enough, raising two babies is even harder. Can you take the challenge and help the mum to take care of her twin babies? The twin babies are sick and you have to take them to the hospital to see the doctor. When they come back you can take them to play puzzles and drawing games. Its time to eat. Please feed them and dress them in cute clothes and then take them for a hot bath.
