How to play

Prism Pulse is a fast-paced timing game where precision meets color. Launch your ball from Point A toward a shifting target ring at Point B. The ring cycles through vibrant colors, and your goal is simple: release at the perfect moment so your ball matches the active color on impact. As levels progress, the target begins moving horizontally and vertically, pushing your reflexes and prediction skills to the limit. Clean, challenging, and endlessly addictive - can you master the pulse?
