How to play

Poppy Playtime is a first-person horror-puzzle adventure game developed by MOB Games Studio, which consists of a small team directed by Isaac Christopherson. Poppy Playtime divided into multiple chapters around the abandoned Playtime Co. factory, specializing in producing “the nation’s favorite toys.” As a former employee at the toy factory, in Poppy Playtime you uncover the mystery behind the disappearances of employees. You progress further into the mysterious factory with your Grabpack while you sneak around the dangerous toys like Huggy Wuggy ahead. The Poppy Playtime game has garnered an abundance of hype throughout the community, and rightfully so! Poppy Playtime introduces horrifying toys and a detailed hidden backstory surrounding the mystery behind the Playtime Co. factory. It uses the popular mascot horror trend used in many horror games but effectively brings chills to the players. With its approach to the Poppy Playtime game, this could easily be one of the most popular game series yet, and one of the best ones to come out this year. Poppy Playtime first chapter is available for PC. Warning: This is not the official Poppy Playtime game. To play Poppy Playtime you need official app or game.
