How to play

It's a busy time in Dress Up Run. Walk along with the platform and pick out the best matching. Well, you drop into a new world that fashion is your work. Tops, skirts, shoes, these things seem normal and easy but you can only win while you are racing through the finish line and picking the correct outfits. Swipe left and right to navigate your way through difficult obstacles and acquire as many diamonds as possible. Check out what happens if you mix and match different colored clothing. It's all about how you dress up or down, so aim for your best appearance at the end!
