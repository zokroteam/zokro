How to play

&quot;Arcade Soccer Pong&quot; mode added. Now you can play ping-pong with obstacles - namely, rocks - on the field! Game becomes unpredictable and challenging. Or you can collect coins, to get best score. Or you can mix it. Anyhow, this definitely changes your pong-ball experience to better and will add more fun to the game. Go to &quot;Options&quot; and visit &quot;Game Mode&quot; tab: use scrollbar to pick either original pong-ball mode or arcade taste. By default, &quot;classic&quot; ping pong soccer mode is selected. When selecting &quot;arcade&quot;, you will get an option to also adjust flavor - if to have rocks on the field (and how many of them) and if to show coins, available for collection. To pick coins, make sure the ball hits it.
