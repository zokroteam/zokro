How to play

Crystal Diamond is a fun 2D puzzle game where players match and collect shiny diamonds in a colorful world. The goal is to line up three or more of the same diamonds to clear them and score points. As players move through levels, the puzzles get harder, making them think carefully to solve each one. The game has bright graphics, catchy sound effects, and special power-ups that add to the excitement. Crystal Diamond is perfect for anyone who loves puzzles and wants to enjoy hours of entertainment. Can you gather all the diamonds and become the top player?
