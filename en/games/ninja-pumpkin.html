🏆 Top players

How to play

You play as a Ninja Pumpkin (yes, it is a kind of ninja!) and in each level, you have to do your best to reach the level’s end. You have to be careful not to fall or touch enemies along the way. There are 3 different kind of enemies in the game. The red ones are the most common one and can be killed jumping on their head or tackling them. The yellow ones can be killed in the same way, but be careful because they jump every few seconds! And the third enemies are the most difficult ones. These black enemies are invincible and can’t be killed and you just have to jump over them and avoid touching them in any way. There are many coins in each level and by collecting 30 coins, you will get an extra life.
