How to play

Welcome to Fruit Frenzy, the ultimate puzzle adventure that will delight your senses with vibrant colors and addictive gameplay! In this fruit-themed matching game, your goal is to swap and match fruits like strawberries, grapes, carrots, apples, and more to create combinations of three or more. Each successful match clears the fruits from the board, allowing new ones to drop in, creating endless possibilities for combos and high scores.
