How to play

Grab your fishing rod and get in your boat, it's time to catch some fish! Many different creatures are living in the deep - now you only need patience and skill. Cast your line and wait for the animals to bite, but beware of the hungry sharks! Try to achieve each level's goal and collect useful power-ups which ascend to the surface. If you need to boost your score, use a bomb to blast a huge amount of fish at once.
