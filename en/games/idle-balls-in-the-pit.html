How to play

Create different kinds of balls and gain money as they bounce around and pit in. Accumulated balls in the pit are going to be used in the boss battles. Point and click the buttons to create &amp; upgrade your balls. The more you upgrade: -the stronger your balls are in the boss battle -the more money you get from bouncing &amp; pitting Use siphon and shaker properties to change things around. Click the green dollar to gain some money. Click “i” for ball info. Tips: -Upgrades have key role in boss battles -When you are sure you can’t beat the boss, retreating is the best option.
