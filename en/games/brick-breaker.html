How to play

Move the paddle with your mouse, arrow keys, or finger. Bounce the ball up to smash the bricks.

Don't let the ball fall past your paddle. Clear all bricks to win the level.

Tips to win

  • Aim for corners to split the bricks
  • Keep the paddle centered
  • Catch the ball early on fast returns

FAQ

How do I move on mobile? Drag your finger to slide the paddle.
