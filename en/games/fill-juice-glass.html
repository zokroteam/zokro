🏆 Top players

How to play

Draw the line and fill the fruit juice glass, initially glass is sad and your task is draw freely line to filled up glass with fruit juice and make happy glass. Its a puzzle game, you can use your logic and fill the glass. Try to find best way and draw a less line to complete each level, you can check our hint option to fill up the glass with a perfect ways and get 3 starts, you can use your mind to fill the glass with best way. There are different fruit juice available such as Orange, Mango, Pomegranate, Watermelon, Pine Apple and Chikoo.
