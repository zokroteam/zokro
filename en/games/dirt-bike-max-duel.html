How to play

The bikes are ready to be ridden on the dirt ground with the Dirt Bike Max Duel game. You will not only ride on the dirt ground but also you will ride on mountains, obstacles, stones, woods, or anything that comes to your mind. You can embark on a very long adventure or you can start a duel against your friend. Your goal is to get to the finish line in time or before your opponent. You can duel in two player mode against time or against round winning amount. You can switch to different bikes after you earn some amount of money. Lots of chapters with fun physics and with cool dirt bikes will be waiting for you!
