How to play

Stupid Zombies Online is a 2D puzzle game that lets you enjoy a thrilling zombie shooting adventure. You have to use your strategic thinking and shooting skills to eliminate all the zombies at each level. You can control the shooting angle and use a limited number of bullets to clear the zombies. The game has 100 levels of zombie-filled challenges for you to complete. You can earn scores by killing all the zombies with as few bullets as possible. If you are ready for some fun and exciting zombie action, then play Stupid Zombies Online now!
