How to play

Its 2024 and dinosaurs are not only extinct, but they live peacefully with humans in the city. Well, not quite peacefully! In this realistic dinosaur game, you can choose from different species of these giant animals and you can move, run, destroy, smash, jump, disrupt and literally any action you want in the free 3D world. There are different levels to unlock and different missions to complete in each level. You also have the option to select your favorite dinosaur from the available species.
