How to play

Turn a shopping cart into a rocket-powered ride! Race along colorful 3D obstacle tracks, smash barriers, launch off ramps and chase a new distance record. Collect coins to upgrade speed, rocket boost and handling. Unlock eight themed worlds, four vehicles, colorful styles and helpful pets. Toy City, Candy World, Water Park, Dino Jungle and space-themed tracks bring new scenery and challenges. Quick single-player runs, arcade steering and spectacular crashes make every attempt a chance to go farther. Progress saves automatically in your browser.
