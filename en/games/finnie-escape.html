How to play

Finnie Escape is an exciting underwater puzzle adventure where you guide a little fish through mysterious ocean mazes. Move across grid-based paths, avoid dangerous enemies, interact with the environment, and use special abilities to survive. Each level introduces new obstacles and tougher challenges that test your logic, strategy, and timing. Find the safest path and help Finnie escape the deep ocean!
