How to play

Stack Tide is a physics-based stacking game where every block matters. Drop and rotate drifting shapes, build the tallest tower you can, and stay ahead of the rising tide. Missed pieces fall into the water, the sea rises faster, and one bad drop can bring everything closer to the end. With simple controls and endless replay value, every run becomes a tense battle between balance, timing, and pressure.
