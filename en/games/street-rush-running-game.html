How to play

Street Rush, save future street &amp; subway destroyed by Big Boss with a runner squad! From the markers of Subway Princess Runner comes Street Rush! Remember to navigate your way and flexibly avoid danger! Street Rush Features: ★Fun level design Rush on tough but bustling subway street!
