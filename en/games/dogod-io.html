How to play

Dogod is a multiplayer action game based on the concept of weapon evolution. Become the god of the dogs in this massive deathmatch arena. Unlock more than 50 cosmetics, skins, hats and pets. HOW TO PLAY MOUSE - Move your character LEFT CLICK - Boost STRATEGY Kill your opponents in this dog fight arena.
