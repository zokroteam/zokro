How to play

Mutant Love Pins is a fascinating puzzle game that tests players to link different characters and make new species with needle-pulling. The best part of this game is the fusion that happens when two individuals meet, making a special creature! But players have to avoid obstacles, like bombs and arrows, and pull the pins in the right order. Dodge traps and help these characters create more wonderful species are essential to win this game!
