How to play

Protect your farm from relentless waves of mutant pests in this action-packed defense shooter! Your quiet farmland is swarmed by strange and dangerous creatures, and only your guns and quick reflexes can keep them away from your crops. Blast through incoming enemies and pick up temporary power-ups along the way &amp;mdash; rapid-fire shots, explosive ammo, health refills, or extra energy for your electric fence. Survive enough waves and the stage ends, giving you a choice of random upgrades: unlock new weapons, call in a crop duster airstrike, slow down enemy hordes, attract power-ups with a magnet, and more.
