How to play

I do not want to go to class, I want to run out of the classroom secretly. Prevent being discovered by the teacher, you open the door to sneak out from the classroom you succeed. This is a very fun escape game, you need to use a variety of props to escape the classroom, not only to avoid all kinds of traps, but also to avoid being found by the teacher! How to play It's very simple: just tap whatever catches your attention and use items. If you get stuck on a puzzle, you can get a hint by watching a video ad.
