How to play

Boom Push is an addictive casual puzzle game. The game is simple to play, gather your opponents to push bombs with you and your task is to destroy each other's castles. Gather as many people as you can and if your opponent outnumbers you, then the bombs will blow up your castle. There are three chances in each level, if you have priority over your opponent and put three bombs into each other's castle, then you will succeed. Come and join in and have fun!
