How to play

Test your focus and split-second reactions in Reflex Pong. Control dual paddles independently to keep the ball moving through a progressive, 3-tier wave arena. Survive an intense 2-minute countdown as the pacing shifts from relaxed coin collecting to a frantic, red-flashing sudden death filled with unpredictable angle mutators, speed variations, and deadly spikes. Deploy shields to block damage, collect multipliers, and manage score deductors that instantly subtract from your total. Can you master the chaos and lock in the ultimate high score before time runs out?
