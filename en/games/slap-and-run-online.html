How to play

As a simple 3D avoiding arcade game, Slap And Run requires you to slap as many colorful stickman walkers as you can and arrive at the finish line while you are avoiding traps on the track at each level. After you overpass the finish line, you could strike an orange stickman to get high scores! Keep calm and have a good time!
