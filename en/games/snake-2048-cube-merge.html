How to play

Each absorbed cube increases your mass, making the snake longer and stronger. But be careful &amp;mdash; colliding with a larger cube will result in defeat. The game offers a unique gameplay experience that blends fast-paced action with strategic thinking. You must plan your path carefully, avoid dangerous cubes, survive as long as possible, and score the highest number of points.
