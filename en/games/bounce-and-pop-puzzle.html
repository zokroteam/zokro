How to play

Thorn and Balloons is a very interesting casual bounce ballon game. In the game, you need to control the strength and angle to launch the thorn ball. The thorn ball will bounce when it hits the wall, and all the balloons will be broken by rebounding to win. Shoot Balloon: Spike Bounce is an exhilarating and fast-paced arcade game that combines precision with rapid reflexes. In this game, players are challenged to pop as many balloons as possible using a set of bouncing spikes. The objective is simple yet addictive: aim and launch the spikes in such a way that they hit and burst the balloons scattered across the screen.
