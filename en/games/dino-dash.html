How to play

Press Space, tap, or press Up to jump. The dino runs on its own and gets faster every second.

One hit and it's over. Time your jumps late and short-hop the small cacti.

Tips to win

  • Jump late, not early
  • Watch the speed — it keeps rising
  • Stay calm at high speed

FAQ

How do I play on mobile? Just tap the screen to jump.
