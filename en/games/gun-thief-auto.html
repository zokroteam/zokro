Game Overview Gun Theif Auto is an open-world voxel action game featuring a 6x6 urban grid. Explore freely, complete mission contracts, and evade police in sandbox or mission modes. Vehicles &amp;amp; Arsenal: Over 15 vehicle classes (sports cars, police cruisers) and weapons including handguns and RPGs. Characters: Over 100 dynamic NPC types, including civilians, police, and rival gangs.
