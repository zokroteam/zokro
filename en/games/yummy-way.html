How to play

Yummy Way – colorful dynamic arcade game. The main character, a whale, swims in the depths of the ocean and eats plankton. Collect series of plankton of the same color to get more points! Dodge enemies! A series of 10 planktons of the same color will give a bonus of protection against the enemy and double the series points of the bonus color!
