How to play

Welcome to the shopping paradise! Explore the supermarket world with many popular mini-games. Help your customers shop and get ready for some fun! This supermarket has a lot of sections: a cash register, departments for grocery, cheese and salami, fruits and vegetables, sweets, and toys, a recycling area and other. Do all the necessary tasks and provide superb shopping services to meet your customers’ needs. • Cash register: Have fun scanning items and issuing invoices. Learn about numbers and make sure to get the right amount of money, just like a real cashier. • Grocery: Keep your eyes peeled in a fun game with hidden objects where you must find foods and drinks like a cake, lollipop, chocolate, juice, spaghetti and many more. Help your customers find what they need.
