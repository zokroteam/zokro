How to play

Here is a small game about a new spider-man. Having chosen a hero or his girlfriend, you have to complete two tasks. The first mission will take you to the subway, where you will jump from train to train, collect coins and dodge enemies, and in the second task you will have to help the hero get down from the roof of a skyscraper for a while.
