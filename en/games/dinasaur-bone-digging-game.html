How to play

Educational and entertaining collection of mini games, including puzzles, fishing and bone digging and cleaning games. Interactive map and creative gameplay enable fantastic user experience. Features : ** There is 4 ways to collect bones and solve the dino puzzle: 1.DIGGING UP BONES – dig up holes and excavate dinosaur bones! 2. FISHING FOR BONES – fish out fossils from the ocean! 3. EXPLORING THE CAVES – use a flashlight to discover the bones in the dark caves! 4. CLEANING THE BONES – clean the bones using special tools and then arrange the bones into a dino puzzle! ** MINI GAMES – 2 exciting mini games you can play in order to proceed to the next level, once your dinosaur puzzle is completed!
