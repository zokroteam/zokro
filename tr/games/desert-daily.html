Nasıl oynanır

You start with 100 water. Heat rises every 10 seconds and evaporates your water. Use 3 buttons: Drink (+8 water), Shade (-heat, costs water), Rest (+score but +heat).

Survive the full 60 seconds to win. Same challenge for everyone today - compare scores with friends.

İpuçları

  • Don't drink early - save score for the last 20 hot seconds.
  • Use Shade right before heat crosses 50 degrees.
  • Rest only when water is above 60.

SSS

Is Desert Daily free? Yes, free forever on Zokro, no signup.

Does it change daily? Yes, the heat pattern changes every day, same for all players.

Can I share my score? Yes, press Copy result and paste it anywhere.
